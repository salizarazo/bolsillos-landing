App information

  • Version: 1.0.0
  • Platform: iOS (iPhone)
  • Requirements: iOS 15.0 or later

Report a problem

If you found a bug or something isn't working right, send us an email at soporte@bolsillos.app with:

  • Description of the problem
  • iPhone model
  • iOS version
  • Screenshot (if possible)

Cancel subscription or request refund

Subscriptions are managed through Apple:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name > Subscriptions
  3. Select Bolsillos Pro
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription

To request a refund, visit https://support.apple.com/HT204084.
