Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

1. Information we collect

Bolsillos does NOT collect personal information. All your financial data is stored exclusively on your device.

2. Data storage

Your data is saved locally on your iPhone, protected with bank-grade encryption (AES-256 via SQLCipher). Nobody — not even us — can access your information.

3. Third-party services

The only external service we use is RevenueCat to process in-app subscription payments. RevenueCat only receives the information necessary to manage your subscription (anonymous Apple purchase identifier). It does not receive your financial data, transactions, or personal information. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy on their website.

4. Analytics and tracking

We don't use analytics. We don't use tracking cookies. We don't use tracking pixels. We don't sell or share data with third parties. There is no user profile on our servers.

5. Backups

If you enable iCloud backups (Pro feature), your data is stored in your personal iCloud Drive account, protected by Apple's security. We have no access to these files. Local backups are stored on your device.

6. Children's data

Bolsillos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will update the “last updated” date on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to us at support@bolsillos.app.
