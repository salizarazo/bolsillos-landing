Frequently asked questions

We answer your questions

Is it safe to use Bolsillos with my money?
Yes, 100%. All your information is stored locally on your phone, encrypted with AES-256. There’s no server of ours that stores it, no cloud, no tracking. Not even we can see your data.
I already tried Excel and failed. Why would this work?
Because Bolsillos does the heavy lifting for you. You don’t have to create formulas or maintain a spreadsheet. Log an expense in seconds — even with your voice — and the app does the math.
I’m a freelancer with irregular income. Does it work for me?
It was designed exactly for that. You can log irregular income, assign what came in to the envelopes you need, and always see clearly how much you have available to spend guilt-free.
Can I try Pro for free?
Yes. With the monthly plan you get 15 days free. With the annual plan, 3 months free. No credit card required to start.
Is it only for iPhone?
For now, yes — it’s available only for iOS. Android is on its way — leave your email below and we’ll let you know when it’s ready.
What happens if I lose my phone?
With Pro, you have automatic iCloud backup that you can restore on any iPhone. With the Free plan, you can manually export your backup and save it wherever you want.
Does it connect to my bank?
Not yet. Manually logging expenses (or with voice) is part of the experience — it keeps you aware of your money. Open Finance integration is on the roadmap for 2027.
