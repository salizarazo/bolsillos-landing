About Bolsillos

The story

Bolsillos was born from a real frustration: personal finance apps aren't made for Colombia. YNAB charges $15 dollars a month and doesn't understand biweekly pay. Nequi lets you see your balance but doesn't help you organize it. And Excel… well, Excel works until it doesn't.

As a Colombian freelancer, I needed something that understood my variable income, helped me separate my PILA (social security) money before spending it, and didn't make me feel guilty every time I bought a coffee.

So I built it.

Who I am

I'm Sergio, Colombian, independent developer and the person behind Bolsillos. I'm not a bank or a fintech with 50 employees. I'm a person who wanted to solve his own money problem and decided to share the solution.

Philosophy

Bolsillos is local-first: your financial information stays on your phone, encrypted, and never leaves. We don't sell data, we don't use analytics, we don't track anything. Your money is your business.

We believe organizing your money should feel as natural as breathing. No guilt, no financial jargon, no spreadsheets.

Bolo

This is Bolo — a zen capybara who accompanies you while you organize your money. Celebrates when you save, reminds you when you forget to log an expense, and hates Excel almost as much as you do.

Founding community

Bolsillos was shaped by 50 people who trusted an unfinished app and gave their honest feedback. Every feature, every visual adjustment, every phrase in the app bears the mark of these founders. Without them, Bolsillos would be just another generic app.

Vision

We want every Colombian — freelancer, salaried, entrepreneur — to be able to answer with peace of mind: “Can I afford this?” No mental calculator, no fear, no knot in the stomach.
